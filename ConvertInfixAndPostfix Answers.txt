2. Why did we choose to use a Stack instead of Queue structure to evaluate the
postfix expression?

A stack is chosen for a postfix calculator because of its LIFO nature. LIFO allows the program to match operators with the two most recent operands.
 A queue's FIFO structure would need to be heavily modified with temp variables to simulate the same program.
